Streamlined Process

How the Smart System Works

A synchronized four-party workflow designed to eliminate emergency search delays and simplify donor management.

1

1. Register & Profile

Donors create a verified profile specifying blood group, recovery interval, and location. Blood banks log verified inventory batches.

2

2. Smart Algorithmic Match

When an urgent or routine request is placed, our rule-based compatibility engine matches ABO/Rh compatibility, distance proximity, and donor cooldown.

3

3. Connect & Transfuse

Donors accept invitations in one click, appointments are logged at licensed centres, and patients receive life-saving blood without delay.

Value Proposition

Why Smart Blood Donation Matters

Addressing fragmented communication, delayed response times, and expired blood inventories.

Zero Wasted Time in Emergencies

FIFO Inventory & Expiry Alerts

Blood banks monitor stock thresholds, shelf lives, and expiry dates to avoid discarding rare units.
